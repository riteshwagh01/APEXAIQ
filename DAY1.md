# What does ApexAIQ do?

## What does ApexAIQ do?

ApexAIQ is a Software as a Service (SaaS) IT Asset Assurance and Cyber Risk Management platform.

It helps organizations discover, monitor and assess all of their IT assets across on-premises, cloud & co-located environment to reduce operational & cybersecurity risk.

### What does it do?

- Discover & monitor all IT assets (servers, devices, software & cloud resources.)
- Identifies security vulnerability, outdated sys & compliance issue.
- Assign risk score & help IT team to prioritize fixes.

---

## Industry problem it solve.

### 1. Lack of assets visibility

Organizations often do not know every device or application running on their environment.

Unknown assets create security & compliance risks.

### 2. Cybersecurity risk

Identifies vulnerable sys & rank them by business risk.

### 3. Technology obsolescence

Outdated hardware & unsupported software increases maintenance cost & security exposure.

### 4. Compliance challenges

Maintaining compliance with internal policies & industry regulation.

### 5. Poor IT decision-making

Provides clean, enriched, near realtime data for better planning & prioritization.

# What does it mean by Agentless?

## What is an Agent?

An agent is a small software program that must be installed on every computer, server, or device to collect information and send it to the management system.

---

## What is an Agentless Platform?

An agentless platform does not need to install any software on the devices.

Instead, it collects information remotely using:

- Existing protocols
- Application Programming Interfaces (APIs)
- Network connections

---

## Why is Apexa an Agentless Platform?

Apexa is agentless because it is designed to discover and monitor technology assets without installing software on each device.

It uses existing technologies such as:

- Network scanning
- Secure Remote Management Protocol
- Existing system credentials
- Cloud Application Programming Interface (API)

### Benefits

- Faster deployment
- Easier maintenance

# Cybersecurity

## Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, software and data from unauthorized access, damage, theft and disruption.

The goal is to ensure that digital systems remain:

- Secure
- Reliable
- Available

---

## Key Cybersecurity Principles

### Confidentiality

Only authorized users can access information.

### Integrity

Data remains accurate and cannot be altered without authorization.

### Availability

Data and systems remain accessible when needed.

---

## Importance of Cybersecurity

- Protects sensitive and confidential data.
- Prevents unauthorized access to systems.
- Ensures business continuity and minimizes downtime.
- Reduces financial losses caused by cyber attacks.
- Maintains customer trust and organizational reputation.

---

## Role of Asset Management in Cybersecurity

- Maintain a complete inventory of all devices and software.
- Identify outdated or vulnerable systems.
- Detect unauthorized or unmanaged devices.
- Prioritize security risks based on asset importance.
- Improve compliance with security policies.

# Important Concepts

## 1. ApexaIQ Score

The ApexaIQ Score is a risk score that measures the overall health and security of an organization's technology assets.

It is calculated using factors such as:

- Vulnerabilities
- Compliance
- Maintenance status
- Obsolescence

A higher score indicates a healthier and more secure technology environment.

---

## 2. IT Asset Management (ITAM)

IT Asset Management is the process of tracking, managing, and maintaining technology assets such as computers, servers, software, and cloud resources throughout their lifecycle—from purchase to disposal.

---

## 3. Vulnerabilities

A vulnerability is a weakness in software, hardware, or a network system that attackers can exploit to gain unauthorized access or cause damage.

---

## 4. Obsolescence

Obsolescence means a technology asset has become outdated because newer versions are available or the manufacturer no longer supports it.

---

## 5. Compliance

Compliance means following industry regulations, security standards, and organizational policies to protect information and systems.

---

## 6. Maintenance

Maintenance is the regular updating, repairing, and monitoring of technology assets to ensure they remain secure and function properly.

---

## 7. End-of-Life (EOL)

The manufacturer stops producing and selling the product.

---

## 8. End-of-Support (EOS)

The manufacturer no longer provides technical support or security updates.

---

## 9. End-of-Maintenance (EOM)

Regular maintenance updates and bug fixes are discontinued.

---

## 10. Asset hygiene

It is the practice of keeping technology assets secure, updated, properly configured & free from unnecessary risk.

---

## 11. Crown Jewel

Crown jewel are an organization's most valuable & critical digital assets, such as customer database, financial sys, Intellectual property & sensitive business information.

---

## 12. Inventory

It is the complete list of all technology assets owned & used by an organization, including their location, status & configuration.

---

## 13. National Vulnerability Database

It is the publicly available DB maintained by United States government that contains information about known cybersecurity vulnerability & their severity/seriousness.

---
## 14. Patch Management

It is the process of identifying, testing & installing software updates that fixes security vulnerability, bugs & performance issues.

---

## 15. Data Breaches

A data breach occurs when unauthorized individual gain access to the confidential information.

---

## 16. Managed Service Provider (MSP)

It is a company that remotely manages another organization's technology infrastructure, security & IT services.

---

## 17. Device Types

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Servers
- Mobile devices
- Printers
- Network devices
- Virtual machine
- Cloud resource
- IoT devices

---

## 18. Software as a Service (SaaS)

It is a cloud based software that users access through the internet without installing it locally.

The service provider manages:

- Updates
- Security
- Infrastructure

**Example:** Microsoft 365

---

## 19. Inbound & Outbound Integration

### Inbound Integration

Data is received from external system into your system / Application.

### Outbound Integration

Data is sent from the Application / your system to other system.

---

## 20. Compliance Standard

Compliance standard are established rules, regulations & best practices that organizations must follow to protect data, maintain security & meet legal or industry requirements.

### Common Compliance Standard

- Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency: provides cybersecurity guidance & best practices.
- International Organization for Standardization: International standard for information security management system.

---

## 21. Perimeter

It is a boundary between an organization's internal network & external network such as internet.

It is protected using firewalls & other security controls.

---

## 22. Auto Remediation

It is the automatic detection & correction of security or system issue without requiring manual intervention.

---

## 23. Due Diligence

It is a careful assessment of risks, security practices, compliance & technology before making business & security decisions.

---

## 24. Security Orchestration, Automation & Response (SOAR)

It is a cybersecurity solution that automates threat detection, investigation & Incident Response by integrating multiple security tools.

---

## 25. Cyber Asset Attack Surface Management (CAASM)

It is the process of continuously discovering, monitoring & securing all technology assets that could be targeted by cyber attackers.

It provides complete visibility into organization's attack surface that helps reduce security risk by identifying unknown, vulnerable or misconfigured assets before attackers can exploit them.
